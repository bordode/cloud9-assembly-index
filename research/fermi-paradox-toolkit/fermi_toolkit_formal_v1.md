### 5. DISCUSSION AND RISK

- **Risk 1:** Linguistic analogies may not constrain physical search spaces. 
  Mitigation: Each lens is operationalized via explicit statistical test; 
  no appeal to linguistic "feel" enters the calculation.
- **Risk 2:** The "wallpaper" approach may be unfalsifiable if any pattern 
  can be retrofitted. Mitigation: Predictions H1 and H2 specify exact 
  datasets, test statistics, and thresholds pre-observation.
- **Risk 3:** Publication resistance due to speculative framing. Mitigation: 
  Present as methodological innovation with conservative statistical 
  standards; separate the philosophical framing from the numerical tests.
