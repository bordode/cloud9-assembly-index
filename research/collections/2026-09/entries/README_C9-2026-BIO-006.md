## Overview

Chinese researchers (Bo Song, Yang et al., University of Shanghai for Science and Technology) have detected an anomalous 71 THz vibrational signal in living cells, mouse tissues, and isolated mitochondria. The signal disappears when mitochondrial structure is destroyed, suggesting a quantum phenomenon tied to organized biological architecture.

**Key Finding:** Exposure to 71 THz and 87 THz infrared light increases ATP production by ~10%, while a control frequency (53.7 THz) shows no effect.
