## What is Subhalo?

**Subhalo** is a Cloud-9 research repository bridging:

1. **TNG Subhalo Validation** — Pull, filter, and compute Assembly Index scores for dark matter halos using the IllustrisTNG API.
2. **Quantum Sensing & Computing** — Formal entries for ion-trap spectroscopy, entanglement gates, BEC analog gravity, and quantum bridge integration.
3. **Stellar Astrophysics** — Observational entries for red supergiants, multi-wavelength galaxy galleries, and black-hole feedback.
4. **Critical Thresholds & Resilience** — Philosophical and complexity-science frameworks for understanding phase transitions, structural failure, and the "Purity Paradox" across physical and social systems.
5. **Evidence Framework Documentation** — Auditable entry schemas with multi-agent hypothesis debate (ADVOCATE/SKEPTIC/EVIDENCE/SYNTHESIZER) for evaluating claims from established cosmology (L1) to unfalsified anomalous phenomena (L2/L3).
6. **Bus Integration** — JSON-serialized outputs compatible with the C9 event bus (`c9_bus.jsonl`) for ingestion by the Oracle, Librarian, Hypothesis Debate, and Quantum Bridge modules.
