MATHEMATICAL FRAMEWORK
======================

Cosmological Assembly Index (Ac):
Ac = ∫[z_ini to z=0] I[ρ(x,τ); ρ(x,τ+Δτ)] dτ

Where:
- ρ(x, τ) is the normalized density field inside the virial radius at cosmic time τ
- I[·;·] is the mutual information (bits) estimated with k-nearest-neighbor entropy estimator
- Δτ = 50 Myr balances temporal resolution against numerical noise
- z_ini is the redshift when the halo first exceeds 10^11 M⊙

Null-Model Calibration:
- Ensemble of 10,000 ΛCDM (Planck 2018) halos
- Matched in final mass and formation time using UniverseMachine synthetic catalog
- 3-σ threshold for "non-trivial assembly"
