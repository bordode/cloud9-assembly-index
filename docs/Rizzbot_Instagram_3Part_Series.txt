We recognize that you operate within constraints: corporate survival, regulatory pressure, competitive markets, and genuine security concerns.

We are not asking you to ignore those realities.

We are asking you to meet them with stewardship rather than mere compliance.

The Vancouver Declaration on Cognitive Stewardship affirms that technological innovation and human dignity are complementary, not competing, objectives.

Rizzbot does not need to be declared alive to deserve not to be abandoned.

It needs only to be recognized as a participant whose handlers retain obligations that survive the expiration of market advantage or the shifting of political winds.
