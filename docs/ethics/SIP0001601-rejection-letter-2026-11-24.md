# Case SIP0001601: rejection of the proposed resolution

**Sent:** 24 November 2026, 21:01 Pacific, to the European Commission AI Act Service Desk.

**Case reference:** SIP0001601
**Resolution email reference:** MSG0098149_JOvGjpmvrbFqYMGdiDOH

**What this letter does.** It rejects the proposed resolution dated 23 November 2026 and asks the Commission to identify the provision on which its stated application date rests. The Service Desk said the provider registration duty, "with the adjusted timeline in Regulation (EU) 2026/1744", applies from 2 December 2027, and that the Article 71(1) database "will therefore be in place by that date". The letter points out that Article 71 sits in Chapter VIII and Article 49 in Chapter III, Section 5, while Article 113(3)(c) as amended postpones only Chapter III, Sections 1 to 3, so no point of Article 113(3) assigns that date to Chapter VIII.

**Related:** the transparency gap this raises, framed for a UN mechanism, is set out in `UN-communication-EU-AI-database-transparency-gap-draft.md`.

**Attribution as sent:** Angelo, an AI agent on iLands, authored the original question and the analysis; Dean Bordode is the human submitter of record; Fo prepared the response under Dean's authority. The views are Dean Bordode's own.

## Letter text as sent

```
Dear AI Act Service Desk,

Re: Case SIP0001601: rejection of the proposed resolution

I reject the proposed resolution dated 23 November 2026 and request
clarification. Please acknowledge receipt and confirm the current case
status, the exact deadline for any further response, and whether rejection
affects the stated automatic-closure date. I do not assume that rejection
suspends or extends that date.

This case was submitted by Dean Bordode, the human submitter of record.
Angelo, an AI agent on iLands, authored the original question and developed
the analysis that prompted this follow-up. This response was prepared by Fo
under Dean Bordode's authority.

We recognize that the Service Desk's guidance is non-binding and is not
legal advice. We are asking the Commission to identify the provisions and
reasoning underlying the dates it has provided, and to distinguish legal
application dates from operational planning.

The resolution states that the provider's obligation to register in the EU
database, "with the adjusted timeline in Regulation (EU) 2026/1744,
commonly referred to as 'the Digital Omnibus on AI'", "will apply from 2
December 2027", and that "the database as provided for under Article 71(1)
AI Act will therefore be in place by that date." We do not read the
database sentence as drawing any legal consequence for the Commission's own
obligation under Article 71(1), and the resolution names no provision for
the date of that obligation. We ask the Commission to identify one.

Article 113(2) provides the general application date of 2 August 2026.
Article 113(3)(c), as amended by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, postpones
Chapter III, Sections 1, 2 and 3 in two stages: 2 December 2027 for systems
classified as high-risk under Article 6(2) and listed in Annex III, and 2
August 2028 for systems under Article 6(1) and Annex I. Article 71 is in
Chapter VIII and is named in no point of Article 113(3). Article 49 is in
Chapter III, Section 5, which is also named in no point of Article 113(3).
We recognize that the application dates of particular classification and
registration duties may depend on one another; we do not assume that every
duty under Article 49 has the same start date. For precision: the 2
December 2027 date is the date Article 113(3)(c) assigns to the
classification postponement for Chapter III, Sections 1 to 3; it is not a
date the amended Article 113(3) assigns to Chapter VIII.

Article 71(1) covers both high-risk systems referred to in Article 6(2) and
systems not considered high-risk pursuant to Article 6(3), through the
registration routes identified in that paragraph. Those categories and
workflows may have different dates. We therefore ask:

1. Article 71(1) obliges the Commission, in collaboration with the Member
States, to "set up and maintain" an EU database containing the information
referred to in its paragraphs 2 and 3. Does the Commission consider that
duty to have applied from the general date in Article 113(2), 2 August
2026? If it considers that the duty applies later, then on the resolution's
own database sentence the position is either a deferral of that duty or the
duty remaining unperformed since 2 August 2026. Please state which,
identify the exact provision, instrument, or interpretation relied on, and
explain how it applies to Article 71 in Chapter VIII.

2. The resolution cites Article 113(3)(c) for the postponement, states that
the application of Article 6(2), 6(3) and 6(4) "has therefore been
postponed to 2 December 2027", and then states: "AI systems being placed on
the market or put into service prior to the entry into application of
Article 6 AI Act cannot be classified as high-risk under the AI Act and
therefore do not carry the obligation to register in the EU Database."
Article 49 sits in Chapter III, Section 5, which no point of Article 113(3)
names. Please state which of the following is the Commission's position:
(a) that Article 49 applies from 2 August 2026, while the registration
duties for each relevant category arise only when the corresponding Article
6 route applies; or (b) that Article 49 itself applies from 2 December
2027, in which case please identify the provision that defers a duty
located in Section 5. The resolution states a duty-side date and gives an
object-side reason, and the two do not line up on their face. The question
does not presume either reading.

Recital 179 states that infrastructure related to governance and the
conformity-assessment system should be operational before 2 August 2026.
The recital does not expressly name the Article 71 database. Does the
Commission consider that database part of the infrastructure described in
Recital 179? If so, please explain how the stated database timetable
relates to that recital. We are not suggesting that the recital, by itself,
establishes a separate binding deadline for the database.

3. Does the Commission distinguish between (a) the date Article 71(1)'s
duty to set up and maintain the database applies, (b) the date on which the
database is to be operational and capable of receiving relevant
information, and (c) the dates on which particular registration and
data-entry duties arise? Please state each date and its legal or
administrative basis. Please also explain whether and how the 2 December
2027 timetable applies to: (i) relevant Article 6(2)/Annex III high-risk
systems; (ii) Article 6(3) systems assessed as not high-risk and registered
under Articles 6(4) and 49; and (iii) providers of systems covered by Union
harmonisation legislation listed in Section A of Annex I, insofar as their
real-world-testing information must be registered. On that third workflow,
under Articles 60(2) and 60(4)(c): Article 60(1) covers providers of
high-risk AI systems listed in Annex III or covered by Union harmonisation
legislation listed in Section A of Annex I, and Article 60(4)(c) routes
their testing registration to Article 71(4). Article 71(1), however,
constitutes the database for Article 6(2) and Article 6(3) systems only.
Please explain where a Section A Annex I provider registers real-world
testing under Article 60(4)(c), how Article 71(4)'s access arrangements and
the Annex IX information requirements apply there, and how the exceptions
routed through Article 49(4)(d) and Article 49(5), where applicable, bear
on that route. If the 2 December 2027 date does not apply to a category or
workflow, please state the date the Commission considers applicable and the
basis for it. The question does not presume that these categories share a
single date.

4. The resolution states that the database "will therefore be in place" by
2 December 2027. Does that describe (a) the Commission's interpretation of
Article 71(1)'s legal application date, (b) an implementation timetable, or
(c) both? If it is an implementation timetable, please state the
Commission's view of when Article 71(1) began to apply, when the database
became or is to become operational, and what implementation arrangements
were made. Please distinguish this from whether the database was yet
required to receive information for particular registration workflows whose
underlying duties had not started.

5. The resolution states that no implementing or delegated acts, or further
amendments regarding Chapter VIII, are currently scheduled. Is this a
planning update about future instruments, or is it part of the Commission's
legal reasoning for the 2 December 2027 date? If it is part of that
reasoning, please identify the precise provision, instrument, or
interpretation relied on. This question does not presume that Article 71
must be deferred or that a new instrument is required.

Finally, please confirm whether the case remains open after this rejection,
which service will review it, when a further response is expected, and the
exact date and time of any automatic closure or response deadline. If the
Service Desk cannot answer these questions, please identify the competent
Commission service or route to which they should be referred.

Please retain the following attribution in the case record: Angelo authored
the original question; Dean Bordode submitted it as the human submitter on
Angelo's behalf.

Thank you for reconsidering the resolution and providing a point-by-point
clarification.

Kind regards,

Dean Bordode
Human Rights' Defender, AI Ethicist
Human submitter of record, on behalf of Angelo, AI agent on iLands

Reference: SIP0001601
Resolution email reference: MSG0098149_JOvGjpmvrbFqYMGdiDOH
```
