## Part III: What Physics Forbids (Layer 3)

### 3.1 The "Frequency Only" Fallacy

The theory that "tuning to the right frequency creates matter" fails on multiple conservation laws:

**Energy Conservation:**

$$E = mc^2 = 0.01 \times (3 \times 10^8)^2 = 9 \times 10^{14} \text{ J} \approx 200 \text{ kilotons TNT}$$

A "hum" or acoustic vibration carries negligible energy. Even if perfectly resonant, you cannot create mass-energy from nothing. The First Law of Thermodynamics is not optional.

**Quantum Number Conservation:**
The universe tracks:
- Baryon number (protons + neutrons)
- Lepton number (electrons, neutrinos)
- Electric charge
- Color charge
- Momentum and angular momentum

A rose contains ~$10^{28}$ atoms. Creating one from vacuum requires creating ~$10^{28}$ protons, ~$10^{28}$ electrons, and ~$10^{28}$ neutrons â each with conserved quantum numbers. You need a **reaction channel**, not a frequency.

**No-Cloning Theorem:**
Even if you had the energy, you cannot clone an arbitrary quantum state. A perfect copy of a living human's quantum state is forbidden by linear quantum mechanics.

**Bekenstein Bound:**
The information in a rose-sized volume requires energy equal to the rose's mass to encode. There's no compression algorithm that beats this:
$$I \leq \frac{2\pi R E}{\hbar c \ln 2}$$
