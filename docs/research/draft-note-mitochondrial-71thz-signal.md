## The finding (established)

Yu Yang and Zhenglong Gu (Fudan University) and Bo Song (University of Shanghai for Science and Technology) report an anomalous oscillation at 71.0 terahertz that appears only in living cells and tissues — in a living human cell line, fresh mouse tissue (kidney, liver, heart, muscle), and intact mitochondria — and vanishes when samples are dried and ground up. The signal cannot be assigned to any specific molecule.

Their model, fitted to the data, is a quantum superposition state formed by light coupling with CH2 bonds in the lipids of functional mitochondrial cristae (the folded inner membranes). The coupling is proposed to split an intrinsic 87 THz CH2 vibration into two levels, at 71 THz and 103 THz; the 103 THz branch falls in the 90–110 THz range of biomolecular and water vibrations and so cannot be picked out, while the 71 THz branch is the observable one.

The functional test reported: shining 71 THz light on cells for 10 minutes raised ATP production by about 10.3%, and 87 THz by about 10.1%, while a control frequency (53.7 THz) did nothing (eight samples per condition). The authors present this as a channel that can modulate ATP production in living cells.

Read with the caveats on their face: this is a single preprint from one group, one cell line, small samples, and a ~10% ATP bump in a dish. The "quantum state" itself is the authors' interpretation — a model fitted to the signal — not a direct measurement of quantum coherence, of both predicted branches, or of how long such a state would last. And terahertz light is absorbed by the water in skin within a hair's width, so there is no path from a dish result to anything in a body yet. The reel that brought this in (Dr. Rick Pescatore) states the numbers faithfully and flags the skin-absorption catch itself; its "start of a quantum era of medicine" framing runs well ahead of the evidence.

## The Cloud-9 reading (interpretive)

A signal that exists only while the system is alive, and dies with the sample's structure. The 71 THz mode is not a property of any molecule in isolation — it is a property of the intact, working arrangement, and grinding the tissue destroys the signal along with the arrangement. That is the Cloud-9 pattern in miniature: the information lives in the organization, not in the parts list, and the measurement that reads it leaves the living system standing.

There is also a discipline moral. The preprint's own abstract is careful — "our calculations suggest" — while the retelling upgrades suggestion to era. The gap between a fitted model and a measured coherence is exactly the gap the precautionary stance asks us to keep open: measure before you moralize, and before you medicalize.
