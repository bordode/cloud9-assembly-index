## 2. ISO Contribution to Galactic Dark Matter Density (Horns et al. 2026)

### Key Findings

- **ISOs as baryonic dark matter candidates.** Researchers at the University of Hamburg used a Poisson distribution extrapolation from the single well-characterized ISO 3I/ATLAS to estimate the total ISO population in the Milky Way.
- **Mass fraction estimate:** 13% to 45% of the mass currently attributed to dark matter could be explained by a population of undetected interstellar objects.
