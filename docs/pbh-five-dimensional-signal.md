## Established findings

- Anchordoqui, Bedroya, and Lüst analyze well-established primordial black hole (PBH) production mechanisms — inflation, phase transitions, and cosmic strings — within the Dark Dimension Scenario and find that all viable mechanisms inevitably produce five-dimensional black holes.
- For cosmic strings, the 5D nature holds regardless of formation temperature; quantum gravity constraints in the Dark Dimension framework force the result.
- The paper connects to the KM3NeT high-energy neutrino detection: the observed energy matches the 5D Planck scale, a signature predicted by the scenario.
- 5D black holes evaporate more slowly than their 4D counterparts, with Kaluza-Klein gravitons as a possible dark matter component.
