I am presenting a framework designed to trace human rights protection from initial obligations to final systemic prevention.

By looking beyond procedural compliance, it ensures that mechanisms like remedy and systemic correction actually achieve their protective purpose instead of merely appearing effective on paper.


Finally, the framework demonstrates that the systemic architecture is mature enough to freeze, providing a vital tool for promoting durable human rights protection.
