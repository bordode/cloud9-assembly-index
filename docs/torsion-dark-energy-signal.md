# Torsion gravity: dark energy that changed its sign

*Research note — 2026-10-13. Intended for `docs/` in cloud9-assembly-index. Established findings summarized from press reporting (Knowridge, Phys.org, Oct 2026) of the peer-reviewed paper — verify against the paper itself before citing. The Cloud-9 reading is interpretive and marked as such.*

## The finding (established)

Dark energy is the unexplained ingredient associated with the universe's accelerating expansion. The standard model (ΛCDM) treats it as constant, but the Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument (DESI) — building the largest 3D map of the universe — has strengthened hints that dark energy's influence may change over time.

A 2025 *Physical Review D* paper by a team led by Özgür Akarsu (Istanbul Technical University; with N. Merve Uzun, Bilal Bulduk, Nihan Katırcı, and Antonio De Felice of Kyoto University's Yukawa Institute) explores an alternative to Einstein's gravity: a torsion-based (teleparallel) theory, where spacetime's geometry twists rather than bends. Earlier work on this theory had followed only the mathematical branch consistent with positive dark energy density; the team relaxed that assumption and examined the overlooked branch.

What they found: the theory naturally produces an *effective* dark energy density that was negative in the universe's first few billion years, crossed zero roughly 9.5 billion years ago, and stayed positive afterward — for the same present-day amount of matter, a different expansion history. "Effective" is doing real work here: the gravity modification *behaves like* a dark energy contribution in the equations; this is not a detection of a negative-density substance. The sign change emerges from the theory's structure rather than being inserted as a separate adjustable event, and the model uses six adjustable quantities — the same number as the standard model. Deviations from Einstein's theory are suppressed in dense environments, keeping it compatible with precise solar-system gravity tests while allowing different behavior on cosmic scales.

Not yet tested against DESI's full dataset. An extended version may also bear on the Hubble tension — the persistent disagreement between local and early-universe measurements of today's expansion rate — and future checks include galaxy growth and gravitational lensing, not just expansion history.

## The Cloud-9 reading (interpretive)

The effective-density framing is the move to watch: a change in *geometry* reads, in Einstein's equations, as a change in *substance*. The "substance" of dark energy may be an artifact of assuming the wrong geometry — the message depends on the channel you assume. And the sign change is not tuned in; it falls out of the theory's structure. Like the exoplanet bimodality, the interesting behavior is emergent from the framework, not purchased with extra parameters — six quantities, the same as ΛCDM, so the fit is not bought with freedom.

There is also a screening parable here: the modification hides where matter is dense and reveals itself in the cosmic voids. The deviation is environment-dependent — the signal only appears where the information density is low. Whether that is a deep principle or a convenient mechanism is exactly what the DESI comparisons will decide.
